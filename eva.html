Running on Mars is a lope: the bounding gait Apollo crews used on the Moon. It starts at 1.29 m/s, where walking stops being efficient in Mars gravity, and tops out at 2.3 m/s in a pressurized suit.

Measured: gravity, air pressure, sunlight, Sun and Earth positions (NASA). Planning values, not measurements: boot grip on regolith (0.62), suit jump speed (1.75 m/s), lope top speed and the 8-hour oxygen supply.
