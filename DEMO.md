# Martian Map · demo script (about 2½ minutes)

One story, start to finish: a crew at Jezero plans a Marswalk, checks it's safe, and walks it.

**Before you start:** open the site, then Settings (gear) → "Save Jezero for offline use". Close other tabs. Use
Chrome or Edge on a laptop with a mouse.

| Time | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Loading screen | "Martian Map is Google Maps for Mars, built only on NASA data. The Sun is today's Sun from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory." |
| 0:10 | Enter the map → Mars | "Mars is charted as the 30 USGS quadrangles. Every layer is NASA Trek." |
| 0:20 | Chip: **Plan a Marswalk** → Jezero Crater | "Five steps. First, where are we going: Perseverance's landing site." |
| 0:30 | Step 2: zoom in | "The map stacks NASA color, MOLA elevation and THEMIS heat. Zoom in and it switches to CTX at 6 m and HiRISE at 25 cm: boulders and dunes. The cream line is Perseverance's real 45 km traverse." |
| 0:50 | Step 3: route | "The suggested route follows the rover's first drives, ground a rover already crossed. Drag a stop to change it." |
| 1:00 | Step 4: go / no-go | "Safety first: daylight, dust season, live NASA space weather, oxygen and the walk-back ring all feed one verdict." |
| 1:10 | Step 5: **Walk this route** | "Now we walk it. This is the real ground from NASA elevation and imagery, in Mars gravity: 38% of Earth's." |
| 1:20 | Walk with W, jump with Space | "The path glows to the next stop. The helmet display counts the distance and checks my oxygen margin to get back. My partner, EV2, walks with me." |
| 1:35 | Press **T** | "And the tablet in my hands runs the whole Martian Map. I can keep walking while I check the map, my position, the safety center." |
| 1:45 | Press **V**, then walk to the rover and press **F** | "Third person shows the suit. The rover's grip changes with the ground: watch slip rise in the sand." |
| 2:05 | Back to map; Directions → Rocket hop → **Fly this hop** | "For long distances, a rocket hop on a real ballistic arc: 55 km, 14 km up, under three minutes." |
| 2:20 | | "Plan on the map, walk or drive it on the surface, hop between sites. One system, all NASA data." |

If something goes wrong: the surface walk menu (Esc) has "Back to the Mars map"; the graphics setting "Low" helps on
slower laptops.
